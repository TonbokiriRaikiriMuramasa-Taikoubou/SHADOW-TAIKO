## 🌐 English

A web-based rhythm game inspired by *osu!* and traditional drumming games. Drop any `mp4` video, and it transforms into a playable rhythm track directly in your browser! 

Built primarily for my friends to play, mod, and enjoy.

### 🚀 How to Play
1. Open the game in your browser.
2. Drag and drop or select any `.mp4` video file from your local machine.
3. The game will analyze the audio and generate a drum-style track automatically. Have fun!

### 🎨 Skin & Asset Customization
Feel free to customize the graphics and sound effects! You are welcome to create your own custom skins and share them within our community.
